# Bullshit Golf

Broadcast-quality golf where you cheat. A Three.js demo: two holes, real ball flight, and a course
marshal who is not always looking.

## The idea

The golf is played straight: drag and Magnus lift on the ball, turf that bounces and checks, greens
that break, and a swing you make with the mouse. It is judged on the three things a launch monitor
reports:

- **Path.** Push straight through and it flies straight. Swing out to the right and it draws; cut
  across to the left and it fades.
- **Contact.** Come back through the ball. Wide right catches the hosel, wide left the toe.
- **Tempo.** Three counts back, one through (two to one with the putter). Snatch it and you thin
  it; ease off and you hit it fat.

Then you cheat.

- **After-touch.** Hold `A` / `D` / `W` / `S` while the ball is in the air. Time slows and you put
  spin on a ball you have already hit. It runs on Spin Juice, and there is not much of it.
- **Cheat cards.** Your caddie offers three before every hole: a magnetic cup, a foot wedge, a golf
  cart parked behind the green. Each one makes the marshal more suspicious.
- **The marshal.** His eye is in the top right corner. Cheat while he is looking away and it costs a
  little suspicion. Cheat while he is watching and it costs a lot. At 100 you are disqualified and
  he keeps the prize money.

Play a hole with no card and no after-touch and he calms down.

## Controls

| Input | What it does |
|---|---|
| Hold left mouse, pull back, push through | The swing. How far you pull is the power |
| Right-drag or `A` / `D` | Aim |
| Mouse wheel or `W` / `S` | Change club |
| `A` `D` `W` `S` in flight, or hold left mouse and drag | After-touch: bend, dive, float |
| `F` | Foot wedge (with the card) |
| `M` | Mulligan (with the card) |
| `Esc` | Pause |

## Holes

- **7, Widow's Pond.** Par 3, 175 yards, over water.
- **8, Gator Alley.** Par 5, 507 yards along a river. The alligators are fake. Mostly.

## Develop

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

`npm run build` type-checks and writes the site to `dist/`.

TypeScript (strict), Vite and Three.js. The course, props and textures are generated by the Python
scripts in `bl/`, run headless in Blender; the exported files are in `public/assets/`.
